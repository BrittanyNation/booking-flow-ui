Design notes

Progress you can see

A numbered stepper sits on every step with plain labels — “Choose a tour,” “Date & bikes,” “Checkout” — so the flow never feels longer than it is.

No surprise totals

The price starts on the tour card and the full breakdown travels with the rider in a summary panel on steps 2 and 3. The total at checkout matches the total they saw first.

States, not just screens

Selected, guided, and small-group states use a badge plus a border and word label — never color alone — so the chosen tour is clear on any screen and for any rider.
